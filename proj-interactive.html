AD SOLEM

UNITY GAME JAM PROJECT

In some vague distant future, the world is in ruins and monsters roam the earth.

Desperate and starving, the last vestiges of humanity
create a sentient robot named Pepper in an attempt to save themselves.

Explore the world as Pepper as they attempt to save humanity. Or not.

Ad Solem is an action-adventure RPG created in Unity.

IMLE

UBC EML TEACHING TOOL

Named the IMMERSIVE MATERIALS LEARNING ENVIRONMENT, This project delivers browser-based interactive teaching tools for materials engineering students taking APSC 278 (Engineering Materials) and APSC 279 (Engineering Materials Laboratory).

This includes connecting how the atomic properties of a material (bonding types, crystal structure) affect the macroscopic properties of a material (tensile strength, elasticity).

ROLE: UI/UX Design, aesthetic and interface, 3D modelling
